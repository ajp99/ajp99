## 👨‍💻 About me
I'm a Senior Software Engineer at Truminds with 4+ years building full-stack products. I'm now moving into **AI Forward Deployed Engineering**: sitting close to the customer, turning messy real-world problems into AI systems that work in production, especially in **enterprise and regulated** settings.

- 🎓 **IIT Delhi CEP, Advanced Certificate in AI Forward Deployed Engineering** (Batch 2). Capstone in progress, due April 2027.
- 🧪 Tested internal **agentic AI systems** at Truminds with **promptfoo** evals and **Playwright** browser automation, then reported the gaps to the engineering team.
- 🎯 MCA in AI/ML (Amity, 2023).

- **Customer-facing delivery:** I take client work from requirements to a live deploy, as I did end to end on Alutech.
- **Full stack to production:** Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL and AWS, from first commit to production deploy.
- **AI evaluation and testing:** I test agentic AI systems with promptfoo evals and Playwright browser automation, and report the gaps back to engineering.
